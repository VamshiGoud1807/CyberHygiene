Essential Cyber Hygiene Tips

Strong Passwords

Use complex passwords with at least 12 characters, numbers, symbols, and letters. Use a password manager.

Spot Phishing

Verify sender email addresses, avoid clicking suspicious links, and never share sensitive info via email.

Update Everything

Keep your OS, apps, and antivirus updated. Enable automatic updates for maximum protection.

Enable 2FA

Use two-factor authentication everywhere possible. It's the best defense against account takeovers.
